CALL OF FRUITE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

The Sixth Hammer OOD provides Call Of Fruite (Android package com.call.fruity). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, contact us, or visit this policy page.

Information on your device

The game stores your progress, unlocked fruits and upgrades, in-game currency, preferences, selected arena, player display name and simulated leaderboard information in the app's local storage. This lets the game remember your progress and settings. These game features do not send that information to our servers. You do not need an account or sign-in to play.

Advertising and tracking

The game does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or developer-operated behavioral analytics. We do not sell your personal information or use your local game data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Google Play and your device

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, security, diagnostic and device information under their own settings and policies. Google Play may provide the developer with app performance and crash reports. Device backup services may back up and restore app data depending on your device settings. These platform services are separate from the game's local save features. See Google's Privacy Policy.

When you contact us

If you email contact@thesixthhammer.com, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, resolve issues and maintain necessary support records. Our email service providers process these messages to deliver and store correspondence. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Where applicable, we process support correspondence based on our legitimate interest in responding to requests and maintaining the game, or to meet legal obligations. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable legal requirements. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit information, including your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. We have not added advertising, tracking scripts or analytics to this page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or remove the app, subject to any device backup or restore settings. Clearing storage removes your saved progress. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local save. You can manage backups through your device or backup provider.

To request access, correction or deletion of support information we hold, or to object to or request restriction of its processing, email us. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to data portability and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will handle requests according to applicable law.

Children's privacy

The game's local save features do not require children to provide personal information to us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Security and international services

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google, GitHub and email services may process information in countries outside your own under their respective privacy arrangements and applicable safeguards.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, contact The Sixth Hammer OOD at contact@thesixthhammer.com.
